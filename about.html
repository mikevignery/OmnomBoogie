HELLO, WE'RE OMNOM BOOGIE

Not a big company story. A beginning.

Omnom Boogie is the home for independently built digital products and creative projects. It starts with Lane Log, a swim-tracking app for swimmers and families.

As new ideas become real projects, they'll have a place here too. The goal is simple: make things that are thoughtful, useful, and genuinely enjoyable to use.
